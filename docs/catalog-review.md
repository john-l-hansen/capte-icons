# Full catalog review — 0.2.0-beta.1

60 unique named icons, with no exact source-geometry duplicates. The three approved pilot definitions are unchanged. This is an unreleased catalog preview, not a new artwork approval.

## Treatment and review limits

- Each icon has 12 templates: four sizes × three styles. Existing profiles provide three weights and three grades; palettes remain independent.
- The 57 additions retain source proportions at 20/24/40/48 dp. They use size-specific stroke profiles and detail strokes; they have not received individual optical redrawing. Dense icons such as Measurable Performance need special attention at 20/24 dp.
- Filled styles use explicit contrast roles. Upload and percentage marks use surface clearances so crossing marks remain visible on solid bodies. Arrow Outward has no enclosed body, so its three style choices intentionally share geometry.
- The legacy vehicle-data icon's raw paints now resolve to existing semantic roles.
- Source Figma masters were not rebuilt or renamed. Native validation used temporary imports, all removed.

## Validation

- 12,960 configurations render across both palettes; 6,480 are exposed by the Light-only plugin.
- 32 tests cover rendering, pilot fingerprints, semantic colors, filled contrast, metadata, all-icon replacement, placement and error recovery.
- Chromium checks cover all 60 cards, category/keyword search, SVG downloads, layout and button-driven replacement through the bundled controller harness.
- Native Figma checks cover 48 dp Duotone and 20 dp Filled for every icon at Heavy/Emphasis: successful SVG import, outlined filled paths, zero remaining live strokes, unchanged root dimensions and bound Capte color variables. Targeted checks followed the last four artwork fixes.
- Full installed-plugin acceptance, undo/redo and design approval remain pending. Native probes do not establish an installed-plugin pass.
